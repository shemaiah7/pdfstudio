Get SentinelPDF for your computer

Open PDFs from Finder or File Explorer straight into SentinelPDF, like any desktop PDF app.

Download for Mac Download for Windows

Mac: macOS 12 or later, Apple silicon or Intel. Signed and notarized by Apple.

Windows: Windows 10 or later (64-bit). Windows may show “Windows protected your PC” the first time — click More info, then Run anyway.

After installing, double-click any PDF and choose SentinelPDF, or follow the steps in the app to make it your default.
