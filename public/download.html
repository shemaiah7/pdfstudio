Three steps

  1. 1. Install

    Mac: open the .dmg and drag SentinelPDF to Applications.Windows: run the installer.

  2. 2. Open a PDF

    Double-click any PDF, or drop it on the window.

  3. 3. Make it your default

    Mac: select a PDF → ⌘I → Open with → SentinelPDF → Change All.Windows: Open with → Choose another app → SentinelPDF → Always.
