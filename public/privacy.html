Privacy policy

This policy explains what information SentinelPDF collects, why, who processes it, and how to delete it. SentinelPDF is operated by Melantech Innovations L.L.C. (“we”, “us”).

1. Your documents

PDFs you open stay on your device. We do not upload, store or read them. Text, page images or audio leave your device only when you use an AI feature, and then only to the providers listed in section 3, for that request.

2. What we collect

  • Account: your email address and name or profile picture if your sign-in provider shares them (through Clerk).
  • Subscription: your plan, trial and renewal dates, and a Stripe customer reference. Card details go directly to Stripe; we never see them.
  • Usage counts: how many AI requests, voice minutes and Deep OCR pages you used, to apply plan limits. Not the content.
  • Terms acceptance: which version you accepted and when.
  • Security and site data: bot checks (Cloudflare Turnstile), basic technical logs, and privacy-friendly page statistics (Cloudflare Web Analytics, no advertising cookies).
  • On your device: preferences such as theme are kept in your browser’s storage, and sign-in uses session cookies.

3. Who processes it

  • Clerk: sign-in and accounts.
  • Stripe: payments and billing.
  • Cloudflare: hosting, our server, database and bot protection.
  • Anthropic: AI answers, summaries and Deep OCR, only when you use them.
  • ElevenLabs: Voice, only when you use it.

Each provider handles data under its own terms and privacy policy. AI providers may keep request data for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring.

4. What we don’t do

We do not sell or rent your information, use it for advertising, or share it except with the providers above or when the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

Account and subscription information is kept until you delete your account. Usage counts reset with each day and billing period. Stripe keeps payment records as the law requires.

6. Deleting your information

Open the account menu and choose Delete account to remove your account and usage data and cancel your subscription. You can also email us to ask what we hold about you or to delete it.

7. Browser extension

The optional SentinelPDF browser extension fetches only the PDF you ask it to open, using your browser’s own sign-in, and hands it to the SentinelPDF page in your browser. It stores one setting (whether PDF links open in SentinelPDF automatically) and sends nothing anywhere else: no analytics, no tracking, and the link you opened is never sent to our servers.

8. Health information

SentinelPDF is not designed for protected health information and is not a HIPAA-covered service. Please don’t upload identifiable patient information (see the terms of use).

9. Children

SentinelPDF is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes

We will post changes here and update the effective date. Significant changes will be shown in the app or emailed to you.
